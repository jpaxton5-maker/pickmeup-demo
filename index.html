How a check-in works

Click through these steps while presenting. The phone on the left acts like a real app. All courts and counts are sample data.

Auto Check-In — hands-free, like a parking garage
1

Walk onto a court

The phone enters the court’s ~100-yard geofence. After 5 minutes, the app asks the player to check in.

2

Stay a while

At 90 minutes the app asks if you’re still there. At 2 hours the check-in expires on its own.

3

Walk away

Once you’ve been outside the geofence for 10 minutes, you’re checked out automatically. No tap needed.

Counts on other courts change every few seconds to show real-time updates.
